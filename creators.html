FAQ

Fair questions.

Where do the views come from?

From TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and more, or from your own data: upload a file or send it via API.

What happens when a video goes viral?

Your caps do their job. Set a limit per post, per creator or per week, and nothing goes past it without you knowing.

Will I pay twice for the same video on TikTok and Reels?

No. Cross-posts of the same video are grouped and paid once, unless your deal says otherwise.

Who handles identity checks and tax forms?

We do. youpifi is the merchant of record for the payouts it makes: we check identities and collect tax forms before the first payout, and you get one invoice.

Do creators need to sign up?

Only when it’s time to get paid. They set up once, and every brand on youpifi can pay them from then on.
