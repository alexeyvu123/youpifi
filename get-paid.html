FAQ

Fair questions.

Do I pay anything to get paid?

The company paying you covers talentpay’s fee. Your bank or local provider may charge its own fees for receiving money.

Why do you need my ID and tax details?

The law asks us to know who we pay, and the right tax form protects you and the company. You do it once, and we keep it safe.

How fast will the money arrive?

Some methods take as little as 15 minutes. Bank transfers in some countries can take a little longer. You’ll always see the status of each payment.

Can I change how I get paid?

Yes, any time. The new method applies to your next payment.

Who is my contract with?

With talentpay. We act as the client of record for the company you work with, so your contract, payments and documents all come from one place.

I didn’t get an invite. Can I sign up anyway?

Ask the company you work with to invite you, or send them to talentpay. Payouts always start with an invite from the company that pays you.
