FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, no trial clock. We earn only when you pay creators through youpifi, and that part is up to you.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the public stats of the posts you add, updated every day. If a creator connects their account, we use their exact numbers instead. You can also upload a file.

Do creators need to sign up?

No. You paste links or handles. Creators sign up only if they want to add posts themselves, or when you pay them through youpifi.

Are there limits?

Track up to 100 creators across your campaigns for free. Running something bigger? Write to us and we’ll sort it out.

Can I share results with a client?

Yes. Send a read-only link to the campaign, or download a CSV or PDF.

What happens when I want to pay creators?

Turn on payouts. Your campaigns, creators and deal carry over, and you pay everyone in one click for up to 5% of each payout.
