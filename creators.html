FAQ

Fair questions.

How do you know what each creator earned?

We read results from the platforms you connect, or from a file you upload. Every amount is tied to the post, order or install behind it, so you can check it before you pay.

Do creators need to sign up?

Only when it’s time to get paid. They confirm their identity, add a tax form and choose a payout method once. After that, payouts just arrive.

Who handles tax forms and compliance?

We do. We check identities and collect tax forms before the first payout, and you get one invoice from talentpay for the whole run.

Can I approve payouts before they go out?

Always. We prepare the run, you review it and press pay. Nothing leaves without your approval.

How is this different from influencer platforms?

Influencer tools help you find creators. We take over after the deal is done: rules, counting, paying and paperwork. Many teams use both.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many people you pay and how often. Book a demo and we’ll put a simple number on it.
