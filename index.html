FAQ

What is a contractor of record?

We become the legal client of your contractor. They sign with us, we handle the paperwork and the compliance risk, you get the work.

Who is talentpay for?

Small and mid-size teams in the EU and the US that work with freelancers and contractors abroad.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay $39 for each payment you send, and nothing in the months you don’t send any.

How fast does the money arrive?

1–2 days by card. 1–3 days by SWIFT, SEPA or local rails.
